privacy

Privacy.

Short version: we don’t sell your data. We keep only what we need to ship you a matchbox, remember you on the waiting list, and answer emails.

what we collect

What we collect.

If you join the waiting list, we store your phone number. If you order a matchbox, we store your name, delivery address, email, and order details. If you email us, we keep the email thread. That’s the whole list.

what we do with it

What we do with it.

We use your contact details to send you the things you asked for — order confirmations, shipping updates, a text when a new batch opens if you’re on the list. Nothing else. We don’t profile you. We don’t run targeted ads. We don’t share your data with third parties except the shippers who deliver your matchbox and the payment processor that takes your card.

how long

How long we keep it.

Order records: seven years, because NZ tax law requires it. Waiting-list phone numbers: until you ask us to remove them, or until you ignore three consecutive batch-opens (whichever comes first). Email threads: archived forever unless you ask us to delete.

your rights

Your rights.

Email hello@honeypop.co.nz to ask for a copy of the data we hold on you, to correct it, or to delete it. We’ll respond within 10 working days. No forms, no hoops. Just ask.

cookies

Cookies.

The site uses one functional cookie to remember your country/currency selection. No analytics cookies, no tracking pixels. If we add analytics later it’ll be privacy-safe and aggregated (probably Plausible or similar). We’ll update this page before we do.
