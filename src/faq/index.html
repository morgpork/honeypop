Questions.

Short answers to the things people ask most. Email us anything we missed.

Ordering

Right now Honey Pop ships from New Zealand with free domestic delivery over $40, flat-rate $6 below that. International shipping unlocks alongside our first stockists outside NZ — sign up to the waiting list to be notified.

Refills

Each insert lasts roughly three months for daily users. New inserts are $8 NZD each and ship in a card envelope. The empty insert slides out, the new one slides in. No tools, no mess.

Ingredients

NZ honey, beeswax, coconut oil, jojoba oil. That’s the entire list. No water, no fragrance compounds, no preservatives — the formula is anhydrous so it doesn’t need them.

NZ honey

We work with NZ beekeepers across the country — Mānuka from East Cape, Clover from Canterbury, Kāmahi from Westland, Pōhutukawa from Coromandel, Tāwari from Northland, Rewarewa from West Coast. Each varietal pairs with one of the six flavours.

Stockists

First retail stockists land in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, and Dunedin during April and May. Full list will live on the homepage as it firms up.

Wholesale

Email hello@honeypop.co.nz with the subject Wholesale and we’ll send the line sheet, MOQs, and current batch availability.

Shipping & returns

Free domestic shipping over $40, $6 flat below. Returns accepted on unopened matchboxes within 30 days — open ones we’ll trust you on. Damaged in transit, just send a photo and we’ll replace it.

Allergies

Honey, beeswax. The flavour oils are derived from real fruit (cold-pressed where possible). If you have a tree-nut allergy, the coconut oil is from a coconut, which is technically a drupe — most tree-nut allergies don’t react, but check with your GP if uncertain.

Vegan?

No. Honey and beeswax are core ingredients. Honey Pop isn’t vegan and won’t be — the four-ingredient bet depends on both.

The matchbox

Made from sustainably-sourced NZ pine, slide-out drawer, food-safe lacquered cavity inside. Designed to last decades; ages well; gets better with handling.
