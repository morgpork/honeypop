how it works

Made to keep.

Honey Pop is built to keep. The wooden matchbox is the object you own. The insert is the part that gets used up — and replaced. A new insert is eight dollars and slides in like a sim card.

Four steps, in order: buy the box, use the balm, refill the insert, keep the box.

buy

One matchbox, first insert installed.

The wooden matchbox arrives with its first insert already fitted inside the drawer. The balm is pre-sealed in its tin, the tin sits in the insert, the insert sits in the wood. Open the drawer, twist up the balm, use it. Nothing to assemble, nothing to peel off.

use

About three months per insert.

For daily users, one insert lasts roughly three months. The balm doesn’t dry out between uses because it’s sealed inside its own tin inside the matchbox — two layers of enclosure keep the wax soft and the honey fresh right down to the last twist.

refill

Slide the empty out, slide the new one in.

When the balm runs out, the empty insert slides out of the drawer with a fingernail — no tools, no prising. A fresh insert slides in the same way. Refills are $8 NZD, direct from us, shipped flat in a card envelope the size of a postcard. One-off purchase at launch; a subscription option will follow once we know the cadence.

keep

The box stays. It gets better.

The wooden matchbox ages. A mark at the corner from the day it fell on the kitchen floor. A warp from a week in the bathroom. The insert runs out and gets swapped; the box stays. By the fifth refill it looks like yours.
