Stock Honey Pop.

display

Merchandising.

The matchbox display case ships free with every first order — six-compartment wooden tray sized for the full range. Case is yours to keep. Replacement trays available if you expand into a second location.

A printed one-sheet (the four-ingredient story, the honey varietals, the refill mechanic) sits behind the tray. Customers pick up the box and know what it is before they read the back.
