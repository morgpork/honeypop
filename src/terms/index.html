terms

Terms.

The rules for buying from Honey Pop. Written so a person can read them.

who we are

Who we are.

Honey Pop is a New Zealand business selling honey lip balm in wooden matchboxes. Registered in NZ. When these terms say “we”, that’s who we mean.

orders

Orders.

Placing an order is a binding offer. We accept by shipping. If we can’t fulfil an order — because a batch sold out between your click and our dispatch — we’ll refund in full within two working days.

Prices on the site are in NZD and include GST. Shipping is free over $40 NZD domestic, $6 flat below. International shipping unlocks at a later stage and will be priced separately.

returns & refunds

Returns and refunds.

Unopened matchboxes: returnable within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is at your cost unless the item was damaged or we sent the wrong thing, in which case we cover it.

Opened matchboxes: we don’t hold you to the “unopened” rule. If the balm is wrong for you, email us and we’ll figure it out — swap for another flavour, credit, or refund. The point is the balm you end up with is one you actually want.

Damaged in transit: send a photo to hello@honeypop.co.nz and we’ll replace within five working days.

refills

Refills.

Refill inserts are $8 NZD each, ship in a card envelope, and fit every Honey Pop matchbox. Inserts are single-use; once installed they aren’t designed to be removed intact. Returns on refills only apply to sealed, un-installed inserts.

quality & allergens

Quality and allergens.

Every Honey Pop balm contains honey, beeswax, coconut oil, and jojoba oil plus a single flavour oil per flavour. Full ingredient list on every matchbox and on the ingredients page. If you have a specific allergy, check the page and ask us if you’re unsure.

Honey Pop is not vegan (honey, beeswax are core ingredients). It is not certified organic; we prioritise NZ provenance and small-batch quality over a certification stamp.

liability

Liability.

We stand behind the balm. Our liability is limited to the purchase price of the product unless NZ law says otherwise (Consumer Guarantees Act, Fair Trading Act). Nothing in these terms tries to limit your rights under NZ consumer law — if there’s a conflict, NZ law wins.

intellectual property

Intellectual property.

The Honey Pop name, the matchbox-lid designs, the graphic system, and the photography on this site are ours. Product photography and brand imagery aren’t licensed for third-party use — ask first.

changes

Changes.

We may update these terms. If the change materially affects you (e.g. refund policy), we’ll email or publish on the site before it takes effect.
