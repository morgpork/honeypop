# HoneyBum — Brand Story

## The Story

The lip balm aisle is two things: expensive natural brands that take themselves very seriously, and cheap petroleum tubes that do not take you seriously at all. Somewhere between the $18 organic ceremonial cacao lip ritual and the $2 thing that tastes like candle wax, there is an obvious gap. A lip balm that is genuinely good, made from four ingredients you can pronounce, priced like it respects your wallet, and designed like it belongs in your pocket on purpose.

HoneyBum is a honey lip balm. NZ honey, beeswax, coconut oil, jojoba oil. That is the ingredient list. The whole thing. We are not adding ceramides or peptides or whatever is trending this quarter. Four ingredients is not a limitation. It is the point.

The name is the joke. The product is dead serious. The tube is the design object. Each flavour gets its own colour stack, inspired by Prada Mode event posters. Bold, saturated, stacked horizontal bands. No illustration, no mascot, no clutter. You see a HoneyBum tube and you know what it is from across the room.

Six flavours at launch. Vanilla, Pomegranate, Coconut & Pear, Beeswax Original, Honey & Grapefruit, Mango. Classic range, done better. Around $7 a tube. The kind of price where someone buys two because the colours are good.

If this works, NZ has a lip balm brand with the conviction of an activist, the price of Kobee's, and design that beats both.

## Audience Truth

"I want something that actually works, costs less than a coffee, and does not lecture me about my carbon footprint while I put it on."

## Founder Truth

Lip balm should not need a philosophy degree. Make it from honey, make it look great, price it fairly, and get out of the way.

## Territory

Cheeky simplicity. Radical honesty about what is in the tube.

---

## Voice and Tone

### The voice

Playful, direct, confident. Talks about the product with the casual certainty of someone who knows the ingredient list by heart because there are only four things on it. Never precious, never preachy. The humour is dry, the quality is not negotiable.

### Attributes

- **Cheeky, not juvenile** — The name is funny. The copy can be funny. The product quality is never the joke.
- **Simple, not simplistic** — Four ingredients is a design decision, not a cost-cutting exercise.
- **Direct, not aggressive** — Says what it is, what is in it, what it costs. Moves on.
- **Designed, not decorated** — Every visual choice is deliberate. The restraint is the flex.

### Word palette

honey, beeswax, four ingredients, tube, colour, balm, pocket, simple, real, NZ made, bold, stack, flavour, smooth

### Off-limits

- Clean beauty, wellness, self-care ritual, nourish, hydrate deeply, infused
- Luxe, artisanal, handcrafted (unless literally true and relevant)
- Any ingredient not actually in the product
- Generic AI filler

### Register

- UK English spelling
- Contractions are fine. The voice is casual.
- "We" for the brand. Never third-person.

---

## Brand Line

**Four ingredients. One tube.**

Speculative. Captures the radical simplicity that is HoneyBum's entire bet. Works on packaging, works as a social caption, works as an answer to "what makes this different."

## Differentiator

The constraint is the brand. Four ingredients, full stop. Every other lip balm brand adds things. HoneyBum's entire identity is built on not adding things. NZ honey is the hero ingredient, the tube design is the hero object, and the price says this is for everyone.
