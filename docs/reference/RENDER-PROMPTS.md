# HoneyBum — Render Prompts

AI-generated product scene renders. Each flavour gets its own NZ landscape world.

## Art Direction

- **Style:** Hyper-lush, saturated, dreamlike — Ghibli-meets-real-NZ
- **Composition:** Tube oversized and centre-frame, dominating the scene like a monolith
- **Lighting:** Golden hour, warm dappled sunlight, soft shadows
- **Camera:** Low angle, shallow depth of field, tube in sharp focus
- **Tube design:** Cylindrical lip balm tube with three horizontal colour bands (Prada Mode style), rounded bottom, small cap. "HONEYBUM" in bold serif on the top band. Flavour name in clean sans on the middle band.

## Notes

- These prompts are written for Midjourney v6+ but work with Flux/DALL-E with minor adaptation
- The tube should always be the largest object in frame
- Maintain the three-band colour system on every tube — this IS the brand identity
- NZ landscapes and native flora are the differentiator — don't use generic tropical scenes
- The vibe is premium but accessible — beautiful but not pretentious
